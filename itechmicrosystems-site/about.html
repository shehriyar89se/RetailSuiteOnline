About us

ITECH MICROSYSTEMS

ITECH MICROSYSTEMS (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED is a software solutions company. We design, build, and support the systems organizations use to run their day-to-day operations.

Our approach

Every organization works differently, so we don't start with a fixed template. We take time to understand how your team actually operates, then design software that fits that reality — rather than asking your team to adapt to generic software.

We stay involved after launch too. Software needs change as a business grows, and we treat support and maintenance as part of the job, not a separate add-on.

What we value

  • Clarity — clear scope, clear timelines, no surprises
  • Reliability — systems built to keep working, not just to demo well
  • Partnership — we work with you through the full lifecycle, not just the build
